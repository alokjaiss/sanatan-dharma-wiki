**Atman** (*ātman*; आत्मन्) is the self. The Upaniṣads identify [[brahman]] with the self (*ātman*) and suggest that knowing it saves one from all sorrow.[^1] For [[advaita-vedanta|Advaita Vedānta]], the essential core of the self is brahman, and the self is pure, non-intentional consciousness.[^2]

[^1]: “Hindu Philosophy”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
[^2]: Neil Dalal, “Śaṅkara”, *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2021).
