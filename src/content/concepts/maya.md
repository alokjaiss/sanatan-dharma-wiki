**Maya** (*māyā*; माया) is, in [[advaita-vedanta|Advaita Vedānta]], the creative power through which [[brahman]] appears as the manifold objects of experience.[^1] *Māyā* is that which appears real at the time of experience but has no ultimate existence.[^1]

[^1]: Sangeetha Menon, “Advaita Vedanta”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
