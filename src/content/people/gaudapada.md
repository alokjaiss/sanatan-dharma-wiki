**Gaudapada** (*Gauḍapāda*; गौडपाद), of the sixth century CE, composed the *Gauḍapādakārikā* (“Verses of Gauḍapāda”) on the Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad; according to tradition he was the teacher of [[govinda-bhagavatpada|Govinda]], Śaṅkara's teacher.[^1] His work is considered the first complete Advaita text.[^2]

[^1]: Neil Dalal, “Śaṅkara”, *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2021).
[^2]: Sangeetha Menon, “Advaita Vedanta”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
