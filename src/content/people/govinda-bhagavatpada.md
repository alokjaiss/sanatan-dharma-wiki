**Govinda Bhagavatpada** (*Govinda Bhagavatpāda*; गोविन्द भगवत्पाद) was the teacher of [[adi-shankaracharya|Śaṅkara]]; according to tradition, Govinda's own teacher was [[gaudapada|Gauḍapāda]].[^1] Govinda and Gauḍapāda are the only two names in Śaṅkara's lineage with some historical certainty.[^2]

[^1]: Neil Dalal, “Śaṅkara”, *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2021).
[^2]: Sangeetha Menon, “Advaita Vedanta”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
