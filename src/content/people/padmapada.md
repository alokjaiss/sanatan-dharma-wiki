**Padmapada** (*Padmapāda*; पद्मपाद) was one of [[adi-shankaracharya|Śaṅkara]]'s direct disciples.[^1] His *Pañcapādikā* comments on Śaṅkara's commentary on the first sūtras of the [[brahma-sutra|Brahma Sūtras]], and a later commentary on it, Prakāśātman's *Vivaraṇa*, gave its name to the Vivaraṇa school of Advaita.[^2]

[^1]: Neil Dalal, “Śaṅkara”, *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2021).
[^2]: Sangeetha Menon, “Advaita Vedanta”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
