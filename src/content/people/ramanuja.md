**Ramanuja** (*Rāmānuja*; रामानुज) was born in the small township of Śrīperumbūdur in the Tamil-speaking south, and the Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy gives his dates as 1017 to 1137 CE.[^1] He wrote nine works, all in Sanskrit, among them the *Śrī Bhāṣya* on the [[brahma-sutra|Brahma Sūtras]] and a commentary on the [[bhagavad-gita|Bhagavad Gītā]]; his school is [[vishishtadvaita|Viśiṣṭādvaita]].[^1]

[^1]: Shyam Ranganathan, “Rāmānuja”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
