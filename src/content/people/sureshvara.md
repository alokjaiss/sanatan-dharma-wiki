**Sureshvara** (*Sureśvara*; सुरेश्वर) was one of [[adi-shankaracharya|Śaṅkara]]'s prominent pupils and is supposed to have written the *Naiṣkarmyasiddhi*, an independent treatise on Advaita.[^1] According to the [[sringeri-sharada-peetham|Śṛṅgeri Śāradā Pīṭham]], Śaṅkara appointed him the first ācārya of that pīṭha.[^2]

[^1]: Sangeetha Menon, “Advaita Vedanta”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
[^2]: “History”, Sringeri Sharada Peetham.
