**Badarayana** (*Bādarāyaṇa*; बादरायण) is the author of the [[brahma-sutra|Brahma Sūtras]], the “Aphorisms on Brahman”, whom Dalal places around the first century BCE.[^1] The Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy dates the composition of the Brahma Sūtras to the first century CE.[^2]

[^1]: Neil Dalal, “Śaṅkara”, *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2021).
[^2]: “Hindu Philosophy”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
