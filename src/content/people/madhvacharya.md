**Madhvacharya** (*Madhvācārya*; मध्वाचार्य), or Madhva (1238–1317), originated the [[dvaita-vedanta|Dvaita]] or “dualist” school of Vedānta in 13th-century South India.[^1] He taught *pañcabheda*, a fivefold difference: between Viṣṇu and selves, Viṣṇu and matter, selves and matter, one self and another, and one form of matter and another.[^1]

[^1]: Valerie Stoker, “Madhva”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
