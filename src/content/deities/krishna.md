**Krishna** (*Kṛṣṇa*; कृष्ण) is one of the two lead characters of the [[bhagavad-gita|Bhagavad Gītā]]: Arjuna's royal cousin, who offered to be his charioteer, and whose teaching on their chariot makes up the dialogue.[^1]

[^1]: “Bhagavad Gītā”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
