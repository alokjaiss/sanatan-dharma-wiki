**Dvaita Vedanta** (*Dvaita Vedānta*; द्वैत वेदान्त), the “dualist” school of Vedānta, originated with [[madhvacharya|Madhva]] in 13th-century South India.[^1] It distinguishes two orders of reality: the independent (*svatantra*), which is brahman alone, and the dependent (*paratantra*), which consists of selves (*jīva*) and lifeless matter (*jaḍa*); its community's main centre is at Uḍupi in Karnataka.[^1]

[^1]: Valerie Stoker, “Madhva”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
