**Mimamsa** (*Pūrvamīmāṃsā*; मीमांसा) is one of the six āstika darśanas; on its view the Vedas are an eternally existing, unauthored corpus.[^1] Its root text, Jaimini's *Mīmāṃsāsūtra*, opens with the enquiry into [[dharma]].[^2]

[^1]: “Hindu Philosophy”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
[^2]: Jaimini, *Mīmāṃsāsūtra* 1.1.1, GRETIL e-text.
