**Vishishtadvaita** (*Viśiṣṭādvaita*; विशिष्टाद्वैत) is usually translated “qualified non-dualism”, or “non-duality of the qualified whole”.[^1] In [[ramanuja|Rāmānuja]]'s account, brahman stands to all other beings as the soul stands to its body (*śarīra* and *śarīrī*).[^1]

[^1]: Shyam Ranganathan, “Rāmānuja”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
