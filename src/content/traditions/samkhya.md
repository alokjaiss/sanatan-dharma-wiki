**Samkhya** (*Sāṅkhya*; साङ्ख्य) is one of the six āstika darśanas.[^1] It provides the metaphysical framework of the [[yoga-darshana|Yoga]] darśana: two distinct categories, *prakṛti*, the primordial matter of the universe, and *puruṣa*, the innumerable conscious selves within it; Sāṅkhya pursues their separation by reasoning, Yoga by meditation.[^2]
