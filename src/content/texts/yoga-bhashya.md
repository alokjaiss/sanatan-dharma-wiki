The **Yoga Bhashya** (*Yogabhāṣya*; योगभाष्य) is the first extant commentary on the [[yoga-sutra|Yoga Sūtras]], ascribed to the legendary [[vyasa|Vyāsa]] and typically dated to around the 4th–5th century CE.[^1] Later commentators elaborated on Vyāsa's reading, which came to define how Patañjali is understood.[^1]

[^1]: Edwin Bryant, “The Yoga Sutras of Patanjali”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
