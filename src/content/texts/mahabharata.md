The **Mahabharata** (*Mahābhārata*; महाभारत) is one of South Asia's two main epics; the [[bhagavad-gita|Bhagavad Gītā]] occurs at the start of its sixth book.[^1] The epic presents itself as the work of Kṛṣṇa Dvaipāyana, [[vyasa|Vyāsa]].[^2]

[^1]: “Bhagavad Gītā”, *Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy*.
[^2]: *The Mahabharata of Krishna-Dwaipayana Vyasa*, tr. K. M. Ganguli, Ādi Parva, sections I and LXIII.
