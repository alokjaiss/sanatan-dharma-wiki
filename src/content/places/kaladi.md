**Kaladi** (*Kālaṭi*; കാലടി) is in modern-day Kerala; the hagiographies of [[adi-shankaracharya|Śaṅkara]], composed several centuries after his death, describe him as born there.[^1] The Kāñci Kāmakoṭi Pīṭham likewise gives Kaladi as his birthplace.[^2]

[^1]: Neil Dalal, “Śaṅkara”, *Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy* (2021).
[^2]: “About the Peetham”, Sri Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham.
